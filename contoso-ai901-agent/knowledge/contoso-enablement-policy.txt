FICTIONAL CONTOSO AI ENABLEMENT POLICY - SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DATA

This policy belongs only to the invented course scenario. It isn't Microsoft policy, O'Reilly policy,
or a real entitlement. Every name, list, and channel below is synthetic.

SCOPE OF THE COACH
The AI Fundamentals Coach explains concepts, generates original practice questions, returns a fixed
study session, and records a self-reported exam booking. It does nothing else.

STUDY SESSION LIMITS
The GetStudySession tool returns one fixed 30-minute session.
It accepts one focus: responsible-ai, workloads, or foundry. A missing focus requires clarification.
A successful result carries status ok and a plan. An unrecognized focus returns status unsupported.
The tool doesn't support custom day counts, custom durations, or multi-week plans.
These three focus labels are Contoso teaching shortcuts. They aren't the official AI-901 skill-area
names, which are published in the Microsoft study guide.

MILESTONE RECORDING LIMITS
RecordExamMilestone saves one row to the ExamMilestones demo list and posts one message to the
Certification Milestones demo channel.
It records what the employee said. It doesn't contact an exam provider, verify a booking, confirm a
seat, or establish that anyone passed.
It runs only after the employee confirms in the current turn. Consent given for anything else doesn't
carry over.
It stores a display name, an exam code, a report type, a timestamp, a duplicate key, and the state of
the announcement. It stores nothing else.

WHAT THE COACH NEVER DOES
It doesn't register employees for exams, issue vouchers or refunds, promise passing scores, verify
identity, grant access, create cloud resources, or contact a mentor unless the instructor configured a
real authorized route.
It doesn't accept or store confirmation numbers, payment details, identity documents, passwords, or
access tokens.
No real email address, booking system, or support queue is provided by this fictional policy.

OWNERSHIP
A named Contoso enablement owner reviews whether answers are supported and whether access is
appropriate before any pilot. Contoso's rule is that the pilot decision belongs to that person, not to
an evaluation score.

Study plans are suggestions. Employees choose whether to follow them and use official Microsoft
sources for certification requirements.
