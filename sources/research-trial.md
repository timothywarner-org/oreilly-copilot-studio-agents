## Exact documented individual steps

1. Open [the official sign-up page](https://go.microsoft.com/fwlink/?LinkId=2107702).
2. Enter the organizational work or school email and select **Next**.
3. Follow the instructions presented for that account.
4. After completion, open Copilot Studio and inspect the approved environment available to the identity.

Do not invent verification-screen labels after Next. A signed-in account with existing access may not show enrollment. Use a genuine sanitized capture if that prevents demonstrating the actual new-user path.

If a personal address is rejected, use a work/school account. If self-service is disabled, contact the organization's administrator after class. Learners are not expected to obtain administrative permission during the session. Do not bypass policy, create an unrelated tenant or purchase a subscription as a class dependency.

## Duration precision

The fetched access article **does not state the initial trial-license duration**. It documents a **30-day extension at expiry**, notification/email reminders, and that an agent can continue working for up to 90 days after license expiry. That is not permission to promise 90-day retention: the same user may be using a trial environment that expires and deletes its contents after 30 days.

Teach learners to inspect their actual license notices and environment expiry separately. Do not repeat “60 days” from memory or equate environment lifetime with trial-license duration.

## Publishing and course format

Trial creation and test-panel use are supported; trial publication is not. A Teams-only included plan is not a substitute for the demonstrated generative-orchestration experience. The instructor uses an appropriately licensed, approved environment for the scoped publishing demonstration.

Trial activation and independent authoring are optional follow-up. All scheduled prediction and decision exercises work without an account. The [Module 1 guide](../instructor/01-inception-guide.md) allocates nine minutes to this walkthrough.
