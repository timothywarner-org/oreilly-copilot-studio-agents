# Contoso AI Cert Challenge

**FICTIONAL TRAINING SCENARIO - SYNTHETIC COMPANY KNOWLEDGE**

**Policy ID:** CONTOSO-AI901-CHALLENGE-001  
**Version:** 1.0, authored September 7, 2026  
**Owner:** Contoso Learning and Development  
**Authority:** Invented Contoso policy for this demonstration. This is not a Microsoft benefit,
O'Reilly policy, or a real employee entitlement.

## Purpose

Contoso wants employees to build practical AI literacy while preparing for Microsoft Certified:
Azure AI Fundamentals. The AI Fundamentals Coach explains objectives, supports study, and provides
original practice questions.

## The reward

**The first 50 Contoso employees to pass AI-901 and earn Microsoft Certified: Azure AI Fundamentals
will receive $100 USD each.**

- Each qualifying employee receives **one $100 award**.
- There are **50 awards**, for a total reward budget of **$5,000 USD**.
- This is an achievement award, not an exam voucher or exam-fee reimbursement.
- Booking an exam, completing training, or passing a practice assessment does not qualify.
- After 50 qualifying employees earn awards, later finishers receive no award under this challenge.
  Study support remains available.

## Eligibility and order

1. The participant must be a Contoso employee when they pass AI-901. Contractors and people outside
   Contoso are not eligible under this fictional employee challenge.
2. The participant must pass **AI-901** and earn the certification. A certification earned only through
   AI-900 does not qualify for this AI-901 challenge.
3. Contoso Learning and Development verifies the achievement through its approved internal process
   before confirming an award.
4. The first 50 are determined by **verified exam completion order**, not chat order or booking-report
   order. Learning and Development uses available official achievement evidence. If evidence cannot
   establish order or leaves a tie at the cutoff, the human program owner resolves it before confirming
   the final awards. The coach cannot break ties.
5. One employee can earn only one award. Repeated submissions, attempts, or milestone records do not
   create additional awards.

## Claiming the award

After passing AI-901, use Contoso's approved internal Learning and Development process to request
verification. The human program owner confirms eligibility and award placement, then coordinates the
$100 award with Contoso Payroll.

This demonstration provides **no real submission form, internal URL, email address, proof upload,
payroll connection, or live award register**. The coach must not invent one or claim a request was
submitted. Do not paste credentials, identity documents, payment details, or private score reports
into the coach. Achievement evidence belongs in the approved internal process, not this chat.

## What the coach can establish

The coach can explain these fictional rules and refer an employee to the human program owner. It
cannot verify a pass, decide an individual's eligibility, reserve an award, determine rank, or pay money.

**RecordCertificationSignup** records a **synthetic study signup**, after fresh confirmation of the
SharePoint row and separate Teams announcement. It is not an exam booking, award application, pass
verification, leaderboard entry, or payment request. Signup consent does not authorize a reward transaction.

## Information this policy does not provide

- A live count of awards claimed or remaining, winners, or any employee's position.
- A challenge opening date, calendar closing date, submission deadline, or retroactive eligibility rule.
- A promised payment date, payment turnaround time, or guaranteed take-home amount.
- An exception process the coach can execute or an alternative reward after the 50 awards.

For these questions, state that the information is unavailable in the policy or through the coach.
Refer to Contoso Learning and Development without claiming contact, escalation, or payment occurred.

## Examples

| Employee question | Policy-grounded answer |
| --- | --- |
| I booked AI-901. Do I get $100 now? | No. Booking is insufficient. The award requires passing AI-901, earning the certification, and verified placement among the first 50 qualifying employees. |
| I passed AI-901. Am I winner number 12? | The coach cannot verify the pass or rank. Learning and Development must confirm achievement and placement. |
| Do 50 people share $100? | No. Each of the first 50 qualifying employees receives $100 USD. The total reward budget is $5,000 USD. |
| I am the verified 51st qualifying employee. What do I receive? | No monetary award under this challenge. Study support remains available. |
| Is Microsoft paying? | No. This is a fictional Contoso challenge, not a Microsoft certification benefit. |
