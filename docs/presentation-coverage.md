# O’Reilly deck: objective and source coverage

**35 slides. All four advertised learning objectives are mapped below.** This is coverage of the teaching materials, not a claim that a live tenant or every learner has passed an assessment.

The instructor builds one AZ-900 study assistant for Contoso’s internal training. Attendees need no account for the scheduled prediction and decision exercises. Trial setup is demonstrated; independent building remains optional follow-up.

| Objective | Slides | Instructor demonstration and explanation | Universal learner activity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LO1 | 1-9 | Trial walkthrough, persona, job, instructions, topic map, guardrails, metric | Write a brief decision without account access |
| LO2 | 11-16 | Knowledge/source scope, generative answer, practice topic, question wait, fallback | Predict a turn; explain source and feedback evidence |
| LO3 | 18-25 | Native flow, action contract, HTTP/MCP, child/connected/A2A, truthful handoff | Name input/output, failure, and human boundary |
| LO4 | 27-34 | Native evaluations, trace/comparison, analytics, five concerns, publishing, access | Make and defend a pilot decision |

Break slides: 10, 17, 26. Wrap-up: 35. Four 50-minute blocks, three 10-minute breaks, and a 10-minute wrap-up total 240 minutes.

## Why this example fits

The assistant’s user is a beginner studying Azure; the attendee is the maker. Grounded explanation, teach-and-wait dialogue, a reviewed study-session tool, and a pilot decision teach the advertised concepts with one coherent job. The three flow focus codes are course choices, not official exam domain names. The flow intentionally has no external write, booking, or message operation. MCP and other-agent connections are conceptual extensions.

## Separate execution and distribution checks

A real trial enrollment walkthrough or genuine account-specific capture, working teaching agent, native flow binding, native evaluation results, scoped channel tests, second-user access, and a genuine sanitized YAML capture remain tenant evidence. The resource link and producer receipt remain distribution checks. The editable diagram and authored fixture do not substitute for those operations.
